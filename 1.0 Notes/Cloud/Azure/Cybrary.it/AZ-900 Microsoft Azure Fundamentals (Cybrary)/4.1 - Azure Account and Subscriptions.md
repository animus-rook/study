Azure Account
- Associated with a specific identity 
- Name, Contact info, payment details
- Work/School and personal account
- Sign into Azure Management Portal
Azure Subscription
- Logical grouping of resources
- Types
	- Free
	- Student (free)
	- Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG)
	- Enterprise Agreement (EA)
	- Cloud Service Provider (CSP)
Multiple Subscriptions
- Different subscriptions for different departments or units within a team
	- limit resources and control billing
