Private
- All data and applications stored on premises
	- Pros
		- Support and scenario (legacy support)
		- Complete control of infrastructurew and security
		- meet security compliance and legal requirements
	- Cons
		- Significant initial costs
		- Limits to agility
		- Demand for IT skills expertise
Public
- All data and applications stores on a cloud provider
	- Pros
		- High scale and agility
		- Pay as you go pricing
		- No hardware and datacenter maintenance requirements
		- Lower technical skill requirements
	- Cons
		- Cannot support all legacy scenarios
		- May not be able to meet all compliance needs
		- No control over hardware and services
		- Some security requirements may not be met
Hybrid
- Components are stored in a mix of both with communication between components. 
	- Pros
		- Can support any scenario
		- Flexibility of choice for applications 
		- Implement specific security and compliance requirements
		- Take advantages of economies of scale
	- Cons
		- Requires initial investment
		- May be harder to set up and maintain
