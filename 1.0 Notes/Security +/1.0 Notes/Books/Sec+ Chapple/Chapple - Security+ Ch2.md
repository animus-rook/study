## Exploring Cybersecurity Threats
### Classifying cybersecurity threats 
Attributes/characteristics of a threat actor can be broken down into
- Internal vs external
- Level of sophistication/complexity
- Resources/funding
- intent/motivation
### Threat Actors
#### Unskilled Attackers
- script kiddies, less discerning and lot of free tools available
- motivated by trying to prove their skill
#### Hacktivist
- use hacking techniques to achieve some activists goals
- believe motivated by greater good
- skill level vary, same as the resources 
- can be internal or external threats
#### Organized Crime
- appears where ever money is to be made
- common across all groups in organized crime is motive and intent (illegal financial gain) 
- organize crime has been linked to
	- Cyber-dependent crim - ransomware, DDoS, 
	- Child sexual abuse material
	- online fraud
	- dark web activity
- skill levels range from moderate to highly skilled
- have more resources than hacktivist or unskilled attackers
#### Nation State Attackers
- usually defined as Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)
	- usew advanced techniques and are persistent occuring over a length of time
- highly skilled actors
- signifigact resources
- can be for either political or financial gain
- APT's generally conduct own security testing to discover zero day attacks
	- attacks that have not bee heard of before so no prior mitigations known
#### Insider Threat
- when bad actor has authorized access to information and services and used that access illicitly 
- can be of any skill level
- varying motivations
- have upperhand as they are already in system
	- Shadow IT
	- employees use unauthorized it programs to complete work, such as dropbox, grive etc. 
#### Competitors
- coperate espionoge
### Attacker Motivation
- primary motivation for attackers can include
	- Data Exfiltration
	- Espionage
	- Service Disruption - take down critical systems or networks
	- Blackmail/extortion
	- Financial gain
	- political beliefs 
	- ethical hack an authorized systems
	- revenge
	- disruption/choas - cause chaos and disrupt normal operations
	- War
### Threat Vectors and Attack surfaces
- [[Attack surface]] - system or application that contains exploitable vulnerability 
- [[Threat Vector]] - the means that threat actors use to obtain access 
#### Message based threat vectors
- Email phishing campaigns
- Messages via sms or instant messaging platforms
#### Wire Networks
- unsecured ports in public spaces
- unsecured terminals
- network devices
#### Wireless networks
- wardriving 
#### Systems
- poorly configured or outdated software systems
#### File and Images
- files containing malicious payloads
#### Removable media
- media containing malicious executables
#### Cloud
- cloud services with improper access controls
- accidental API keys made public
#### Supply chain
- inserting malicious code prior to devices/software in use at targeted orgs
- MSP breeches are in this category 

## Threat Data and intelligence
- threat intelligence - set of resources/activities used to monitor/learn about changes in threat environment
	- intelligence is also used for predictive analysis to identify potential risks
- Vulnerability databases ?
- [[(IOSs) indicators of compromise]] - tell tell sign that an atack has occured
### Open Source Intelligence
- threat intelligence acquired from publicly available sources
### Proprietary and Closed-source intelligence
- usually developed and maintained by security focues vendors
### Assessing Threat Intelligence
- common factors to look for 
	- Timeliness
	- accuracy
	- relevant
### Threat Indicator Management and Exchange
- STIX (Structures Threat Information eXpression) - XML language used to help with automation
- TAXII (Trusted Automated eXchange of intelligence infromation) - used to help share cyber threat intelligence
### Information sharing organizations
- intelligence communities created to share cyber threat intelligence information
### Conducting your own research
- professionals should conduct research to mainting visibility on current threat landscape
