## 📝 CH 4 Social Engineering and Password Attacks

### Social Engineering and Human Vectors
- Certain principles are leveraged to social engineer an individual
	- Authority - claiming to be authority or knowledgable in a certain situation
	- Intimidation
	- Consensus based engineering (everyone is doing it)
	- Scarcity
	- Familiarity
	- Trust
	- Urgency
#### Social Engineering Techniques
- **Phishing** - fraudulent acquisition of data
	- *Smishing* - phishing via SMS
	- *vishing* - phishing via telephone/voicemail
	- Spear phishing - phishing attacks that go after a specific target
	- Whaling - attacks that target "big fish" CEo, etc
	- most common defense against phishing attacks is awareness/user training
##### Misinformation and Disinformation
- Misinformation - bad unverified 'mistake' incorrect information 
- Disinformation - incorrect, inaccurate, intentionally spread to serve an individual or goal
##### Impersonation 
- pretending to be someone else as an individual or representing a company such as a delivery person
##### Business email compromise
- relies on legitimate emails to conduct scams or other attacks 
##### Pretexting
- process of using a made up scenario to justify approaching an individual
##### Watering Hole attacks
- attack that focuses on compromising a specific website that the attacker knows users frequent 
##### Brand impersonation
- uses emails that are intended to appear to be from a legitimate brand, relying on name recognition
##### Typosquatting
- use misspelled or slightly off names of websites
- pharming useses malware to change dns server or host file on victims machines
### Password Attacks
#### Brute force attacks 
- iterate thru a bunch of passwords till one works
#### Password Spraying
- uses a single or small set of passwords against multiple accounts 
#### Dictionary Attacks
- uses a list (dictionary) of words for their attacks
