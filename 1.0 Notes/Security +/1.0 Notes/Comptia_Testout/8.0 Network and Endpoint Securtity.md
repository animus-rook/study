## OS Hardening
- changing the OS/Application to ensure it operates securely
- Balancing act against accessability
- The principal of least functionality
	- functionality should be limited to only what the users need access to
	- Minimizes threat attack surface
		- Network interfaces not in use should be disabled
		- Unused services disabled
		- Unused or unsecure protocol ports should be disableded
		- Disk Encryption
			- Encryption techniques
				- Full disk encryptiopn (FDE)
				- Removable media encyrption
				- VPN
				- Email Encryption

- Implicit deny ACls
